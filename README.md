# Motor Insurance Pricing - Generalized Linear Models and ML
---
This project presents a framework for insurance pricing based on the redacted publicly available datasett from Spanish motor insurance spanning from 2022 to 2024. I build a frequency severity framework based on Generalized Linear Models (GLM) to compute pure premium while also incorporating a Tweedie GLM modelling pure premium directly. The models are evaluated on 2024 out of sample.
